## #23 Tracked document links

**Paid demand:**
- DocSend: no free tier, 14-day trial. Reported pricing varies by source ($10–$180, or "$15"/"$30" starting).
- PDF Deck: free plan with 3 documents; viewer identification only on paid tiers.
- PandaDoc Essentials reported at $19/seat/month.
- Papermark (open core): founders say $75k MRR in an interview; Tiny Startups lists $45k MRR self-reported, updated
  2025-04-19. **Founder claims, not verified.**

**Channel caveat:** Papermark's origin was a founder tweet (~40k views). That is the X marketing this project excludes,
so Papermark proves demand, not the no-marketing channel.

**Young domains in results:** hummingdeck.com (2026-01-21), getpulsesignal.com (2026-02-22), pdfdeck.com (2023-11-22).
Many results are "DocSend alternative" comparison pages written by competitors.

**Risks:** Papermark is free to self-host; Google Drive/Dropbox own the default; crowded "alternative" SEO.

**Possible niche (hypothesis):** a vertical where tracked sending is the job and DocSend is overkill (e.g. freelance
proposals, real-estate listing packs, tutors' worksheets). Untested.

**Math (illustrative, $25/seat/month):** $10.7k–13.3k pre-tax target = 427–533 paying seats.
$100k/month = 4,000 seats.

## #25 Invitations + RSVP

**Pricing:**
- Joy: free core; paid premium designs.
- Greenvelope: one-time by guest count, $19 (≤20) to $99 (100), as reported.
- Rizz Invites: free ≤10 guests, $14.99 (≤50), $29.99 (51–150).
- WedSites: $99/$199 one-time. Lemonvite: $5/event. Evite premium reported $14.99–$99.99.

**Spread:** strongest of the three. Partiful's growth is attributed to invites as marketing touchpoints
(Sensor Tower ~500k MAU Q1 2025, estimate). Partiful is VC-funded and free; Apple Invites launched Feb 2025.

**Young domains:** rizzinvites.com (2026-04-27), thedigitalyes.com (2025-12-29), usecarly.com (2025-07-04).

**Revenue evidence:** AppGoblin estimates most invitation apps at <$10k/month (estimates, unverified).

**Economics:** one-off purchases, no MRR. At $20/event: target = 534–667 paid events/month;
$100k/month = 5,000 paid events/month, every month.

## #16 Niche booking links

Free options everywhere: Cal.com (open source, self-host free), Square Appointments, Fresha (marketplace fee instead of
subscription), Zoho Bookings, Microsoft Bookings (in M365), SimplyBook.me, SchedulingKit, EasyWeek, Koalendar, TidyCal
($29 one-time). Young: usecarly.com (2025-07-04), mykalen.com (2026-06-07, "free forever").
A new entrant competes on price with free. **Reject.**
