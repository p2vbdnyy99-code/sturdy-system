# Outreach templates

Starting points only. Every message opens with **one real observation from the
prospect's own public page** (in `[brackets]`), stays under 120 words, makes no
claim outside `brand-guide.md`, and ends with the opt-out line. Draft with
`ttm stage <id> draft --message "…"`; the founder approves and sends by hand.

Links: build them with `links.build_url("/sample-analysis", source="direct_outreach", medium="email", campaign="<campaign>")`,
e.g. `https://tendertez.in/sample-analysis?utm_source=direct_outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=consultants_oct26`.
Never paste a link copied from a Gmail draft or sent email: Gmail wraps it in a
Google redirect (`link-policy.md`).

## 1. Short introduction (any business)

> Subject: Reading [organisation]'s tender faster
>
> Hello [business name] team,
>
> [Observation: e.g. "Your site lists supply of lab equipment to IIT and AIIMS."]
> I built TenderTez to help with the reading part of a bid: upload a tender PDF
> and it lists the deadline, EMD, eligibility conditions and the documents to
> submit, each with the page it came from, so you can check every point.
>
> The first 5 tenders are free, with no card needed: [link]
> Would you try it on a tender you are looking at this week?
>
> Sayali Londhe, Founder, TenderTez (Mumbai)
> If you would rather not hear from me, reply "no" and I will not write again.

## 6. Follow-up (once, after 5 days, only if no reply)

> Hello [name or team], following up once on my note of [date] about [their
> specific tender type]. If it is useful, reply with any tender PDF and I will
> send you the analysis. If not, no problem, and I won't write again.

## 7. Referral request (to an activated user)

> Thank you for trying TenderTez on [tender]. Is there one other firm or
> consultant you work with who reads tenders often and might find it useful? An
> introduction by email is enough; I will mention you introduced us.

## 8. Re-engagement (signed up, didn't upload; only to users, once)

> Hello [name], you created a TenderTez account on [date] but haven't uploaded a
> tender yet. Your 5 free tenders are still there. If something got in the way
> (sign-in, a scanned PDF, the profile), reply and tell me; I will fix it or run
> the tender for you.
