# TenderTez brand guide (marketing)

## Voice

Plain, specific and useful, like a careful colleague who has read the tender.
Short sentences. Indian English and Indian number formats (₹3,999; 1,23,456 for
lakh amounts when quoting tenders). No hype words, no emoji walls, at most two
hashtags on LinkedIn and none on X unless a community uses one.

Write to one reader: a contractor's owner, a tender manager, or a consultant who
reads tenders for clients. Name them in the first line when you can
("If you bid on GeM as an MSE manufacturer…").

## What we may say (true today)

- Upload a tender PDF from CPPP, GeM or a state portal; TenderTez lists the
  deadline, EMD, eligibility conditions, BOQ and risky clauses, each with its
  page number, so you can check every point yourself.
- It checks eligibility against your company profile and builds a submission
  checklist from the tender's own requirements.
- Corrigendum check, bid tracker with Excel export, EMD and bank-guarantee
  tracking, annexure drafts as Word files, client workspaces for consultants.
- Free search of central, state and GeM tenders from CPPP's public lists
  (not every tender in India).
- First 5 tenders free, no card. Plans from ₹3,999 a month for 10 tenders.
- Built in Mumbai by Sayali Londhe.

## What we never say

- Accuracy or completeness promises ("100% accurate", "never miss a clause").
- That it wins bids, decides whether to bid, or gives a bid/no-bid verdict.
- Time or money saved, unless a named customer told us and agreed to be quoted.
- Testimonials, customer counts or logos we don't have.
- That it fills, writes or files the bid.
- "Best", "#1", "only", "guaranteed".
- Any fact about a tender rule without a source link (GFR, DoE OMs, GeM GTC,
  the tender itself).

The content queue refuses most of these automatically (`ttm lint`).

## Links and tracking

Link to the most relevant public page, not always the home page: a state or
organisation landing page, `/government-tenders`, `/pricing`, `/faq`.
`ttm draft` and the MCP tools tag links with standard UTMs
(`?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=<campaign>`); see
`link-policy.md` and `analytics/kpi-definitions.md`.

## Sign-off

Founder messages: "Sayali Londhe, Founder, TenderTez (Mumbai)". Every first
message ends with an easy opt-out: "If you would rather not hear from me, reply
'no' and I will not write again." Record every 'no' with `ttm stage <id> opt_out`.
