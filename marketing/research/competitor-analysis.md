## Tender information services (indirect)

BidAssist (from ₹3,000/year + per-document fees), TenderTiger (from ₹2,400/year),
TendersOnTime, Tender18, TenderJunction, Tender247, XpressTenders. Several also
sell bid-preparation services, so they are both competitors and possible
customers or partners.

## Substitutes

A general AI chatbot (ChatGPT Go, ₹399/month); a human consultant per bid; staff
reading the PDF in-house.

## Where TenderTez stands

- Scope: reads and checks (page-linked facts, eligibility against the profile,
  checklist from the tender's own requirements, corrigendum check, trackers,
  annexure drafts, client workspaces). It does not write or file the bid and
  gives no go/no-go verdict.
- Price: the highest published price for AI reading (₹3,999 for 10 tenders).
- Differences we can state truthfully: every point linked to its page; a
  checklist built only from the tender's requirements; a corrigendum check that
  quotes the changed text and its page; published prices; free public tender
  search with no sign-in.
- Not a moat: competitors claim similar extraction. The opening is a buyer
  (consultants and bid teams who are judged on not being rejected on form),
  not a feature.
