# Customer personas

Segments and shares come from the founder's planning model
(`founder/customer.json`), which was **tested on a simulated buyer panel, not
real customers**. Real evidence so far is listed under each persona.

## 1. Tender consultant (planning share ~30%)

Prepares and files bids for 5–20 client firms; judged on bids not being
rejected on form. Uses: a page-linked summary and checklist per client tender,
client workspaces, annexure drafts.

Real evidence: one consultancy used it (1 tender) and declined for now
because it is building its own system. Another consultancy signed up and
analysed 1 tender after outreach (names in the CRM, not in Git).
Many consultancies advertise publicly (IndiaMART, own sites), so they are easy
to find and contact.

Reach: their own websites' business email; LinkedIn company pages; IndiaMART
enquiry (manual).

## 2. Small MSME contractor or supplier, owner bids (~45%)

Turnover under ₹5 crore; 3–5 bids a month; owner or one staff member reads
each tender; phone-first. Uses: deadline, EMD (and whether they are exempt),
eligibility check, document list.

Real evidence: one free-trial account with no tender uploaded yet.

Reach: public award notices (CPPP results of tenders) → their own website;
industry associations; educational posts.

## 3. Tender manager at a mid-size MSME (~25%)

Turnover ₹5–50 crore; small bid team; many tenders a month. Uses: bid tracker,
EMD/BG tracking, corrigendum check, checklist, Excel export.

Real evidence: none yet.

Reach: LinkedIn (job titles "tender executive", "bid manager"), associations.

## Who to target first

Consultants: one buyer reads many tenders, the product has client workspaces,
and they are publicly listed with business contacts. Then contractors who
publicly win tenders often.
