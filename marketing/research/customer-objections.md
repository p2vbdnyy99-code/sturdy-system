# Customer objections and honest answers

"Real" means said by a real prospect; "simulated" means from the founder's
simulated buyer panel (`founder/customer.json`), useful as hypotheses only.

| Objection | Source | Honest answer |
|---|---|---|
| "We are building our own internal system." | Real (a consultancy on the free trial, 15 Oct) | Respect it. Offer the free tenders for comparison; don't chase. Ask what their system will do that ours doesn't (product input). |
| "₹3,999 a month is more than my alert subscription costs in a year." | Simulated | Alerts find tenders; TenderTez reads them. Compare with a day of staff time per large tender, in their own numbers. First 5 tenders free. A smaller plan is a founder decision (`strategy.md` item 10). |
| "Can I trust an AI not to miss a clause?" | Simulated | Don't promise it won't. Every point shows its page so you can check; the checklist comes from the tender's own requirements. Try it on a tender you've already read. |
| "ChatGPT can read a PDF." | Simulated | It can summarise. TenderTez checks eligibility against your profile, links each point to its page, builds the checklist, checks corrigenda and tracks bids. Show a side-by-side on one public tender. |
| "I only bid 2–3 tenders a month." | Simulated | The free tenders cover a month or two. Price fit is a real issue at this volume. |
| "I don't want to upload client documents to an unknown site." | Simulated | Tender PDFs are public documents. The privacy policy says what is stored and who processes it; the company profile is optional for the first 2 tenders. |
| "It doesn't fill or file the bid." | Simulated | True. It reads and checks; filing stays with you. Annexure drafts help with formats. |
| "No online payment; I have to wait." | Simulated (and true) | Today: email and same-day activation by the founder. Online payment is a founder decision. |
