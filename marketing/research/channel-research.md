# Channel research

"Verified" means a person or this session opened the community and checked it
is active, relevant and allows the post type. **No community is verified yet.**
Search snippets are leads only, never proof that a group exists.

## How to verify a community (do this before any post)

1. Open it while signed out (or as a member, if it is the founder's own).
2. Note: members or subscribers, last post date, language, whether tender
   questions are asked, and the rules on promotion.
3. Record it in a row here with the date checked. Only then add a post for it
   to the content queue.
