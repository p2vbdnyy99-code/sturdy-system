# TenderTez reel 1: "One clause in 150 pages"

Style: Stick-figure Explainer. Vertical 1080x1920, 60 fps, about 36 s. English voice (Kokoro) and English subtitles. Story is the founder's product, TenderTez.

## Three candidate structures
1. A list that turns: five tips, the last one is the product. Reads as slideware; rejected.
2. Before/after with a chain: a long row of people (the tender's pages) hides one accent figure (the clause that sinks a bid). The bidder can't find it; then a pulse runs the line and the figures that matter step forward with their page numbers. Chosen: the people are the diagram, and the accent has one meaning, the clause that matters.
3. Countdown: days to the deadline shrinking. Pressure without showing what the product does; rejected.

## Logline and arc
A bidder faces a tender too long to read; one clause in it can sink the bid; TenderTez pulls out the points that matter, each with its page.
Setup: one figure, then a row that keeps growing past the frame (150 pages). Turn: one figure in the row turns accent, far from the bidder, and the row hides it again. Ending: the pulse runs the line, four figures step out with labels (Deadline p.4, EMD p.4, Eligibility, Documents), the row turns to face the bidder.

## Benchmark
Learn: one idea per screen, generous space, script carries the logic (stick-figure essay channels); powers-of-ten scale change for "150 pages". Don't take: any character, layout, typeface or music.

## Sound
Kalimba pop for each pop-in (rising), soft sweep for the pulse, glockenspiel on each of the four hits, vibraphone chord at the end. Near-silence at 10 s (before the accent) and at 19.5 s (before the pulse). Voice about 10 dB above music. -14 LUFS.

## Type
Jost-like geometric sans, left-aligned at the margin, one statement per screen. Subtitles bottom-left with a 4 px accent bar, one English line. Accent #EB4A2A marks only the clause that matters and the points TenderTez returns.

## Facts used (all from tendertez.in)
Lists deadline, EMD, eligibility and documents with page numbers; first 5 tenders free; "check a tender in minutes" (guide pages). "150 pages" is said as "can run to".
