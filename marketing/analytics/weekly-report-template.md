# Weekly growth report: template

Produced by `python3 -m ttm report` (or `/weekly-growth-report`, which adds
the commentary). Sections:

1. **Funnel** (verified, from the CRM event log): new qualified prospects,
   contacted, replies, positive replies, demos, first successful use, new
   paying customers, total paying, revenue, opt-outs. Imports of earlier
   contacts are shown separately.
2. **Channels**: contacts and replies by channel.
3. **Content**: drafted, approved, published; best posts by enquiries, then
   clicks, then comments (only where metrics were recorded).
4. **Website**: visitors, sign-ups, first analyses, paid plans, with the source
   of each number (production report, GA4).
5. **Due now**: follow-ups due, items waiting for approval.
6. **Recommended next actions**: computed from the recorded state.
7. **Stop / continue / improve** (written in the skill, from the numbers above,
   labelled as recommendations).

Labels: *verified* (recorded), *not available* (nothing recorded),
*recommendation* (judgement). No estimates.
