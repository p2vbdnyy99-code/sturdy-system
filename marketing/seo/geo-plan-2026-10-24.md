## 1. Executive summary

TenderTez is invisible today, both in search and in AI answers, for every buyer question checked.

- **Brand search:** a web search for "tendertez" and for "tendertez.in" (24 Oct) returned nothing about TenderTez. The search engine treats the name as a misspelling of "tenderize". The brand is not yet a known entity.
- **"Find a tool" questions** (about 12 checked, e.g. "best AI tender analysis software India", "GeM bid document analysis AI tool") are answered from software directories and startup profiles: GetApp, Capterra and Software Advice (Tenders Agent), SaaSworthy, SourceForge and YourStory (BidShakti, TenderKart), Slashdot and TechnologyCounter (Minaions). TenderTez is listed on none of them.
- **"How do I" questions** (eligibility, EMD exemption, corrigendum, documents checklist) are answered from buyers' tender PDFs, court judgments and thin aggregator blogs. Nobody has a clear, sourced explainer. That is the opening TenderTez can win.
- **The biggest problem is on the site itself.** Home, features, pricing, FAQ, about and the tender search send crawlers one word of body text. Their content appears only after JavaScript runs, and GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot generally don't run it. So the pages that describe the product say almost nothing to AI systems: each shows only its title, meta description and (home and pricing) JSON-LD.
- **What already works:** the 47 tender landing pages and `/sample-analysis` are server-rendered with 600–1,700 words, an H1 and correct canonicals. All 56 sitemap URLs answer 200. No AI crawler is blocked.
- **The 30-day plan, in order:**
  1. Server-render the core pages.
  2. Get onto Bing (which ChatGPT search draws on) and onto the 6 directories that answer tool questions.
  3. Publish 6 pages written to be quoted: a fair tool comparison, ChatGPT vs TenderTez, a consultants page, and three sourced guides.
  4. Measure AI citations weekly with the log template.

**Q1 implementation notes:**
- **Generating the HTML.** Prerender the public React pages at build time: `vite build --ssr` plus `renderToString` with a `MemoryRouter`, one HTML fragment per path in `PUBLIC_PAGES`. `renderIndexHtml` (`backend/src/seo.js`) puts the fragment inside `<div id="root">`. `createRoot` replaces it on load, so there is no hydration mismatch to manage, and the strict CSP is unaffected (no inline styles or scripts).
- **Data-driven parts** (live tender counts) render a neutral placeholder.
- **Signed-in users** at `/` are still redirected by the app.
- **Tests:** a test asserts each public path's HTML contains its H1. Extend `seo-audit` to fail on <100 words.
- **Rejected alternative:** a hand-written HTML copy of each page would drift from the app. Avoid it.

### 3.6 What to stop or not start

- **Tender-alert head terms** ("tender alerts", "all India tenders"). Aggregators own them with thousands of pages. Our landing pages stay limited to lists with 20+ open tenders (no doorway pages).
- **"X alternatives" pages for names that are ambiguous or unverifiable.** "BidAssist alternatives" returns a German product.
- **Paid listicle placements or bought links.** Undisclosed placements break the trust the plan relies on.
- **Promotional Reddit posting.** No Reddit thread surfaced for any tender query, so the effort has no proven return; reply only where a real thread asks.
- **Keyword-tool spend** before 4 weeks of Search Console data exists.
- **More `llms.txt` variants.** Their payoff is unconfirmed.
