What links where today (server HTML and the app), and what to add. Website
changes need the founder's approval and a deploy.

|---|---|---|---|
| Home | /government-tenders, state landing pages (chips with 20+ open), /pricing, /register | yes | Sample analysis page (when it exists) |
| Landing pages | other landing pages, /government-tenders search, home, /register | yes | — |

| /pricing | /register, mailto | yes | FAQ answers about price and trial |
| /faq | /privacy-policy, contact | yes | /features sections that answer each question |
| Articles (future) | the landing page and feature each article relates to | — | one or two contextual links each, no link blocks |

Rules: descriptive anchor text ("Maharashtra government tenders", not "click
here"); no link farms; no hidden links; never link to a page that isn't in the
sitemap or is noindex.
