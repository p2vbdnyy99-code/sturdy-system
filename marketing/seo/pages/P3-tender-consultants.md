**TenderTez helps tender consultants check each tender against the right client in minutes:**
- you keep a profile per client firm;
- each tender PDF is read and its eligibility conditions are checked against that client's turnover, experience and registrations;
- each point carries the page it came from.

All your clients share one plan, from ₹3,999 a month for 10 tenders, after 5 free tenders.

## What does a consultant get?

- **A workspace per client.** Add each firm under Clients with its own profile. Choose on a tender who it is for, and the eligibility check uses that firm's details.
- **Every condition with its page.** Deadline, EMD, fees, eligibility, required documents, BOQ items, key dates and risky clauses, each with the page number and quoted text, so you can show a client exactly where a requirement is.
- **A checklist to send the client.** Built only from the tender's own requirements, with pages. Tick off documents as they arrive, then download the list as Excel and send it to the client.
- **Corrigendum check.** Upload the corrigendum on the tender to see what changed in the deadline, EMD, eligibility or scope, quoted with its page.
- **Annexure drafts.** The tender's own forms (undertakings, declarations, bidder details) filled from the client's profile as Word files. Anything the profile doesn't hold is marked "[to fill]". Price bids and BOQs are never drafted.
- **Bid tracker.** Stage, internal deadline and notes per tender, plus EMD and bank-guarantee records, with guarantees expiring within 30 days listed on the dashboard.

## How does it fit a consultant's week?

1. A client sends a tender, or you find one in the free tender search (central, state and GeM tenders from CPPP's public lists).
2. Upload the PDF and choose the client. The analysis usually takes a few minutes.
3. Review the eligibility check: conditions the client meets, appears not to meet, or that couldn't be checked, each with the reason.
4. Send the client the Excel checklist of documents to collect.
5. Upload any corrigendum and update the checklist.
6. Draft the annexures, check them, and prepare the bid as usual. Filing and prices stay with you.

## What it does not do

- It gives no bid/no-bid verdict. You and your client decide.
- It never files a bid or fills in prices.
- Its tender search covers CPPP's public lists, which is not every tender in India.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I manage several client firms in one account?**
Yes. Add each firm under Clients, fill in its profile, and choose the client on each tender. All clients share your plan.

**Do my clients need their own logins?**
No. The client workspaces sit inside your account.

**Can one client see another client's tenders?**
Clients don't log in to your workspaces. Separately, one company account can never see another company's tenders.

**How long does an analysis take?**
Usually a few minutes; very large documents take longer. You can leave the page while it runs.

**Can I send the checklist to a client?**
Yes. Download it as an Excel sheet; each line comes from the tender's own requirements, with its page.

**Does it draft the client's annexures?**
It drafts the tender's own forms as Word files using that client's profile. Anything missing is marked "[to fill]" for you to complete. Price bids and BOQs are never drafted.

**Do I have to fill in every client's full profile first?**
Your own company profile is optional for your first 2 tenders. The eligibility check on a client's tender uses that client's profile, so fill it in before you check their tenders. Fields that don't apply can be answered "None".

**What does it cost if I handle 20 tenders a month?**
The Pro plan covers 30 tenders a month for ₹8,999; Growth covers 15 for ₹5,999.

**Can I try it on a client's live tender?**
Yes. Your first 5 tenders are free with every feature included.
