## Which tool should you choose?

- **You bid on 3 or more tenders a month and get rejected on technicalities:** choose a tool that shows the page for every condition, so you can check it yourself before you spend on EMD and documents. TenderTez is built around this: no point without its page.
- **You prepare bids for several firms:** you need separate client profiles. TenderTez has client workspaces; QuickBid says it supports multiple company profiles.
- **You sell mostly on GeM and want the lowest monthly price:** Tender Sahayak AI's launch plan is ₹399 a month.
- **You want the tool to say bid or don't bid:** TenderTez deliberately doesn't. BidShakti, QuickBid and TenderKart say theirs do.
- **You only read one tender a month:** a free trial (TenderTez gives 5 tenders) or a general AI chatbot may be enough.

## TenderTez

TenderTez is an AI tool that reads Indian government tender documents for contractors, suppliers and tender consultants.

- **What it lists:** you upload a tender PDF from CPPP, GeM, a state portal or a PSU, and it lists the deadline, EMD, fees, eligibility conditions, required documents, BOQ items, key dates and risky clauses. Each point links to its page and quoted text.
- **When it can't find something:** it says "Not found in document" instead of guessing.
- **Pros:**
  - Every point is checkable in seconds.
  - Eligibility is checked against your saved company profile: meets, does not appear to meet, or could not be checked, with the reason.
  - The submission checklist is built only from the tender's own requirements, and downloads as Excel.
  - The corrigendum check quotes what changed and where.
  - Consultants keep one profile per client.
  - Prices are published.
- **Cons:**
  - No bid/no-bid verdict.
  - It never files a bid or fills a price bid or BOQ.
  - Scanned pages are read in English only, up to 15 per document.
  - Online payment isn't available yet; you subscribe by email.
- **See it on a real tender:** [sample analysis of a 35-page AIIMS Bhubaneswar tender](/sample-analysis).

## Tender Sahayak AI

Tender Sahayak AI describes itself as an assistant for GeM Portal sellers.

- **Features its site lists:** an AI bid search, a tender analyzer that extracts criteria, compliance items and deadlines, WhatsApp alerts, a catalogue helper, clarification drafting, a bid-document explainer and a readiness score.
- **Prices:** ₹399, ₹999 and ₹4,999 a month, marked as launch pricing for the first 100 customers.
- **Best for:** GeM sellers who want alerts and plain-language explanations at a low price.
- **Watch for:** its site does not mention page references for each extracted point.

## QuickBid

QuickBid describes an end-to-end platform:
- portal discovery and alerts;

- a go/no-go recommendation;
- annexure extraction and bid document generation;

You pay per AI task with "QB Coins" that don't expire, and its site lists multiple company profiles and unlimited users as free. Coin prices are not published.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI read a government tender accurately?**
It can read most of it, but it can miss or misread things. Use a tool that shows the page and quoted text for each point, so you can check what matters in seconds before you bid.

**Which AI tender tool is free?**
TenderTez analyses your first 5 tenders free with no card. Tenders Agent's listing shows a free trial. ChatGPT has a free tier for one-off questions.

**Which tools give a bid/no-bid verdict?**
BidShakti, QuickBid and TenderKart say they give a go/no-go view. TenderTez does not; it shows each condition and whether your profile appears to meet it, and leaves the decision to you.

**Do these tools work for GeM bids?**
Yes. Tender Sahayak AI is built for GeM sellers. TenderTez reads GeM bid documents you upload, and its free tender search lists GeM bids from CPPP's public lists.

**Can these tools fill in or submit my bid?**
QuickBid says it generates submission-ready bid documents. TenderTez drafts the tender's own annexures as Word files from your profile, never price bids or BOQs, and never submits anything.

**Do they read scanned tender PDFs?**
TenderTez reads scanned English pages with text recognition, up to 15 scanned pages per document. Check each other vendor's site for its limits.

**What about Hindi tenders?**
TenderTez includes Hindi text from typed PDFs in the analysis and shows results in English; scanned Hindi pages aren't read yet.

**Are my tender documents safe?**
At TenderTez, a company's tenders are visible only to its own account. The document text is sent to OpenAI's API for analysis, and OpenAI states that API data is not used for training. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) lists every provider.

**How much should I expect to pay?**
Published prices range from ₹399 a month (Tender Sahayak AI's launch offer) to ₹8,999 a month (TenderTez Pro, 30 tenders). Several tools publish no price.

**What's the difference between a tender alert service and an AI tender tool?**
Alert services such as BidAssist or TenderTiger find tenders for you. AI tender tools read the tender document and pull out what you must meet. Many bidders use one of each.
