# Paid-niche scan and search-funnel reality check (2026-11-03)

## Decision: **PIVOT (narrow), HOLD on building**

- **A generic image converter: NO-GO.** The verified revenue of small, comparable tools is $5–$26 a month.
- **The pattern that does work:** an image tool whose output feeds a workflow people pay for (cutting machines,
  lasers, embroidery). One verified newcomer, VectoSolve, reached about **$95/day** after ~8 months on SEO alone.
- **Before building:** pick a niche that VectoSolve does not already cover, then pass the SERP and pricing check (§5).

All revenue figures below are Stripe-verified figures shown on public TrustMRR pages, read on 2026-11-03.

- **Sample is small and biased:** about 10 public pages, read individually; no bulk collection, no API.
- **Survivorship:** founders choose to list on TrustMRR, so these are not a random sample.
- **Margin of error:** TrustMRR says figures can differ from reality by up to 30%.

**Estimate (assumes about 15% average click-through rate at top-3 positions):** 1,000 visitors a day needs

- I could not get keyword volumes: the paid SEO connectors (Ahrefs, Similarweb) need authorising, and I did not invent
  numbers.
- **Realistic timeline from the one verified case:** VectoSolve made $6.50 in month 4, $969 in month 6 and about
  $2,000–4,300/month from month 7 onward.
- **It has plateaued at about $2,900–3,250/month for 4 months**, the bottom of your target.
- **So "2–4 days" applies only to building. Expect revenue after 6–12 months, and only if the niche is right.**

## 5. Why VectoSolve works and heictojpg doesn't (inference from the two cases)

1. **The output goes into something people already spend on** (Cricut, Glowforge or xTool, embroidery machines). A
   bad free result costs them material, so paying for quality is rational. Converting HEIC to JPG has no such stake,
   and phones and operating systems now do it for free.
2. **Pay-per-use credits fit one-off search visitors**: credit packs from $6.99 as well as subscriptions.
3. **One page per machine or use** (for example /svg-for-laser-cutting and /embroidery-converter) captures many
   transactional queries.
4. **A free watermarked preview** shows the quality before payment.

**The catch:** VectoSolve's own niches are now crowded. On 2026-11-03 it already ranked on Google for
"image to embroidery" and "image to SVG for Cricut/laser", alongside click-stitch, embroidai, Adobe Express, Canva,
picsvg and others. Copying it means arriving late against a site that is about a year old.

## 6. Candidate niches to test next (unvalidated hypotheses, about 30 minutes each)

All follow the §5 pattern. None is verified yet.

| Candidate | Why it might fit |
|---|---|
| Photo → laser-engraving prep (dithering and grayscale tuned per machine and material) | Machine owners already pay; output quality is visible |
| Photo → cross-stitch, diamond-painting or knitting chart | Hobby spending; printable output |
| Print-ready upscaling and DPI fixing for print-on-demand sellers (Etsy, Redbubble, KDP covers) | A rejected upload costs sellers money |
| Sublimation and DTF transfer prep (background removal, mirroring, white-underbase files) | Small print shops buy repeatedly |

**Pass test for each:**

- The Google top 10 contains **at least 2 small or new sites** (domain registered after 2023, checked by RDAP).
- **At least 1 paid competitor** with public pricing.
- An AI answer does not fully solve the job on the results page.
- A browser-side implementation is feasible, so the cost per file is near $0.

**Kill rule:** if none of the four passes, stop the image line and return to the VentureScout pipeline.

## 7. Risks

- **Small sample.** One strong newcomer case (VectoSolve) carries this conclusion; one more failure or success could
  move it.
- **Biased sample.** TrustMRR is self-selected and figures may be off by up to 30%; "visitors" is
  founder-reported analytics.
- **Assumed click-through rate.** The 15% CTR and $8 per customer are assumptions; real keyword volumes are unknown.
- **AI risk.** AI answers and AI image tools (Canva, Adobe, ChatGPT) could absorb these jobs too.
